# My Spanish Syllabus

Curriculum order follows the **Read2Speak Breakthrough (B1–B2)** sequence.
Foundations (A1–A2) sits below it and Mastery (C1–C2) above, for reference.

Format, one topic per numbered line:

    <name> — <covered|next|queued> — confidence <1-5> — layer <accuracy|speed|naturalness|confidence>
        <one-line summary, indented>

## Breakthrough (B1–B2)

1. Ser vs. estar (+ gender agreement) — covered — confidence 5 — layer confidence
    Identity/classification vs. state/result. Resolved in the error log.
2. Future simple + irregular stems — covered — confidence 5 — layer confidence
    Endings on the full infinitive; twelve contracted stems. Future of probability spontaneous.
3. The three -R forms (futuro / condicional / subjuntivo imperfecto) — covered — confidence 4 — layer naturalness
    Recognising and producing the three families that share the -r- signal.
4. Preterite narration — covered — confidence 3 — layer accuracy
    Forms in place; person endings (yo/él) still slipping.
5. Imperfecto vs. pretérito — background vs. foreground — next — confidence 2 — layer accuracy
    How Spanish splits a past narrative into events and the scenery around them.
6. Por vs. para — full treatment — queued — confidence 2 — layer accuracy
    Cause/exchange/duration/means vs. purpose/recipient/deadline/standard.
7. Perfect tenses & pluscuamperfecto in reporting — queued — confidence 1 — layer accuracy
    he entregado / había entregado. Ties directly to the participio error.
8. Relative clauses (que, quien, el cual, cuyo) — queued — confidence 1 — layer accuracy
    The long subordinated sentences that separate B1 from B2 in writing.
9. Pasiva refleja & impersonal se — queued — confidence 1 — layer accuracy
    se entregó, se observaron — the default voice of Peruvian technical reporting.
10. Discourse connectors & argument structure — queued — confidence 2 — layer naturalness
    sin embargo, no obstante, dado que, siempre y cuando — the B2 essay backbone.
11. Subjunctive beyond the triggers (opinion, doubt, concession) — queued — confidence 1 — layer accuracy
    no creo que, aunque + subjuntivo, el hecho de que.
12. Register: negotiating, hedging and defending a position — queued — confidence 1 — layer confidence
    The exam's oral task and the actual job description are the same thing here.
